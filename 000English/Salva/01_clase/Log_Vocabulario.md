# Log de Vocabulario y Dificultades

## 2024-01-01

### Pronunciation — Minimal Pairs
- **shoot** /ʃuːt/ — long "oo" sound, distinct from "shut"
- **shout** /ʃaʊt/ — diphthong "au", distinct from "shot"
- **shot** /ʃɒt/ — short "o", distinct from "shout"
- **shut** /ʃʌt/ — short "u", distinct from "shoot"

### Grammar
- ✅ **"The latch is closed"** — *closed* = adjetivo ("cerrado")
- ❌ *"The latch is close"* — *close* = adjetivo ("cerca"), no tiene sentido con *latch*
- ✅ **"The door is latched"** — *latched* = adjetivo ("cerrado con pestillo")
- ❌ *"The door is latch"* — *latch* es sustantivo, no adjetivo
- *Latch* = pestillo / *close* (verbo) = cerrar / *close* (adj) = cerca

### Vocabulary — Automotive

- **shock absorbent** — ❌ *absorbant* → ✅ *absorbent*

## 2026-07-01

### Vocabulary
- **"Take it up a notch"** — increase intensity of language register (not vulgar)
- **"Go full vulgar"** / **"Switch to vulgar register"** — use taboo language

### Language Registers
- Five registers covered: Formal, Neutral/Standard, Informal/Colloquial, Slang, Vulgar/Taboo
- Key insight: appropriateness depends on context and audience, not just word choice
