### 💬 Example Sentences from Conversation

**Dar largas / tener enganchado (to string someone along)**
- *Mi jefe me está dando largas con el aumento de sueldo.* — "My boss is stringing me along with the pay raise."
- **Note:** *Pay raise* (US) vs *pay rise* (UK) — ambos = "aumento de sueldo"

**Un guiño / una señal para la base (a dog whistle)**
- *Los políticos usan ciertas palabras como guiños a su base.* — "Politicians use certain words as dog whistles."
- **Classic examples:**
  - *"Some people say..."* / *"I think we've had enough of experts"* — Michael Gove (Brexit campaign, 2016)
  - *"Law and order"* — US political dog whistle (Nixon → Trump)
  - *"La España profunda"* / *"el sentido común"* — guiño a la base conservadora en España sin mencionarlo explícitamente
- **Key characteristic:** El que no está en el target no lo nota, pero el target lo capta al instante.

**Un tipo escurridizo / pájaro de cuenta (a slippery customer)**
- *Ese político es un pájaro de cuenta — siempre sale limpio de los escándalos.* — "That politician is a slippery customer — he always comes out clean from scandals."

**Mark / grifter (la víctima / el timador)**
- *No quiero ser el mark de ese grifter.* — "I don't want to be the mark of that grifter."

## 5. Recurring Mistakes / Observations

- **«Obviar»** does **not** mean *to wipe out*. It means *to bypass / omit / avoid*. Careful with false friends.
- **«Mark grifter»** — confusion between *mark* (victim) and *grifter* (scammer). Clarified.
- **«Preposición»** noted next to *string along* — remind to review phrasal verbs + prepositions.
