# 📋 Language Registers — Post-Class Notes (2026-07-01)

## 📝 Vocabulary

- **"Take it up a notch"** — increase intensity of language register (not vulgar)
- **"Go full vulgar"** / **"Switch to vulgar register"** — use taboo language

## 🗣️ Language Registers Covered

| Register | Example |
|----------|---------|
| Formal | *"I believe that politician is mistaken."* |
| Neutral/Standard | *"I think that politician is wrong."* |
| Informal/Colloquial | *"That politician is full of it."* |
| Slang | *"That politico is talking out his arse."* |
| Vulgar/Taboo | *"That fucking politician is talking shit."* |

## 💡 Key Takeaways

- Context and audience determine which register is appropriate, not just the words themselves
- The line between informal, slang, and vulgar depends on the social setting
