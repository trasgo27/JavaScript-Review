### 💡 Pro-Tip for Learning:
Spanish nouns have a **gender** (masculine or feminine). To learn them effectively, always learn the word with its article:
*   **El** (masculine) -> *El libro* (The book)
*   **La** (feminine) -> *La mesa* (The table)

Most words ending in **-o** are masculine, and most ending in **-a** are feminine.
