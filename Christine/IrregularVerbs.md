Here are **30 of the most frequently used irregular verbs in Spanish**, with their meaning and a quick example so you can actually *use* them:

---

### 🔑 Core Irregular Verbs

1. **ser** (to be – permanent)
   👉 Soy estudiante. (I am a student)

2. **estar** (to be – temporary)
   👉 Estoy cansado. (I am tired)

3. **ir** (to go)
   👉 Voy al trabajo. (I go to work)

4. **haber** (to have – auxiliary)
   👉 He comido. (I have eaten)

5. **tener** (to have/possess)
   👉 Tengo un coche. (I have a car)

6. **hacer** (to do/make)
   👉 Hago ejercicio. (I do exercise)

7. **decir** (to say/tell)
   👉 Digo la verdad. (I tell the truth)

8. **poder** (can/may)
   👉 Puedo ayudarte. (I can help you)

9. **dar** (to give)
   👉 Te doy un regalo. (I give you a gift)

10. **ver** (to see)
    👉 Veo la televisión. (I watch TV)

---

### 🔄 Common Stem-Changing / Irregular Forms

11. **saber** (to know – facts/skills)
    👉 Sé nadar. (I know how to swim)

12. **querer** (to want/love)
    👉 Quiero café. (I want coffee)

13. **llegar** (to arrive) *(spelling change sometimes)*
    👉 Llego temprano. (I arrive early)

14. **pasar** (to pass/happen) *(some irregular uses)*
    👉 ¿Qué pasa? (What’s happening?)

15. **deber** (should/must)
    👉 Debo estudiar. (I should study)

16. **poner** (to put)
    👉 Pongo la mesa. (I set the table)

17. **parecer** (to seem)
    👉 Parece fácil. (It seems easy)

18. **quedar** (to stay/remain) *(irregular uses)*
    👉 Quedo en casa. (I stay home)

19. **creer** (to believe)
    👉 Creo en ti. (I believe in you)

20. **hablar** (to speak) *(mostly regular but very frequent)*
    👉 Hablo español. (I speak Spanish)

---

### ⚡ Strong Irregular Verbs

21. **traer** (to bring)
    👉 Traigo comida. (I bring food)

22. **conocer** (to know people/places)
    👉 Conozco a María. (I know María)

23. **salir** (to go out/leave)
    👉 Salgo a las ocho. (I leave at 8)

24. **venir** (to come)
    👉 Vengo ahora. (I come now)

25. **valer** (to be worth)
    👉 Vale mucho. (It is worth a lot)

26. **caer** (to fall)
    👉 Caigo rápido. (I fall quickly)

27. **oír** (to hear)
    👉 Oigo música. (I hear music)

28. **seguir** (to follow/continue)
    👉 Sigo adelante. (I continue forward)

29. **dormir** (to sleep)
    👉 Duermo bien. (I sleep well)

30. **pedir** (to ask for/order)
    👉 Pido comida. (I order food)

---

### 💡 Quick Tip

Most of these verbs are irregular mainly in:

* **yo form** (e.g., *tengo, hago, digo*)
* **stem changes** (e.g., *puedo, quiero, duermo*)
* or **completely unique forms** (*ser, ir*)
