Learning regular verbs is the most efficient way to master Spanish because they follow a predictable pattern. Once you learn the "template" for **-ar**, **-er**, and **-ir** verbs, you can conjugate all 150 of these without looking them up.

Here are 150 of the most frequently used regular Spanish verbs, categorized by their ending.

### 💡 A Note on "Regular" Verbs
Some verbs are regular in the **Present Tense** but might be irregular in the **Past Tense** (Preterite), such as *escribir* (which has an irregular past participle: *escrito*). 

However, for 90% of your daily conversations, these verbs will follow the standard patterns. Focus on the first 20 of each list—they do the heavy lifting in the Spanish language!
