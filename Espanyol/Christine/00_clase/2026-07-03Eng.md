# Pretérito Indefinido vs Pretérito Imperfecto

> Quick guide for **British English** speakers — with mental hooks, tables and practical examples.

---

## 1. Central distinction

| **Indefinido** | **Imperfecto** |
|---|---|
| **Completed** action — has a clear beginning and end | **Ongoing / unfinished** action — no defined limit |
| "It happened and it's done" | "It was happening, but we don't know if it finished" |
| The **result** is visible | The **context / scene** is described |

> 🇬🇧 *Mental hook:* Think of a **checklist tick** ✅ (indefinido) vs a **scene unfolding** 🎬 (imperfecto).

---

## 2. Mental hooks for Brits

### The "I was ___ing WHEN ___ happened" structure

The star combination of spoken British English:

| British English | Spanish |
|---|---|
| **I was walking** to the pub **WHEN I saw** Dave | **Caminaba** hacia el pub **CUANDO vi** a Dave |
| **I was having** a cuppa **WHEN the phone rang** | **Tomaba** un té **CUANDO sonó** el teléfono |
| **We were queuing** **WHEN it started** pissing down | **Hacíamos** cola **CUANDO empezó** a llover a cántaros |

🔑 **Golden rule:** The *was/were ___ing* → **Imperfecto**. The *___ed / happened* → **Indefinido**.

---

## 3. Trigger words

| **Indefinido** (punctual action) | **Imperfecto** (habitual / ongoing action) |
|---|---|
| **ayer** — yesterday | **mientras** — while / whilst |
| **anoche** — last night | **antes** — before (used to) |
| **de repente** — suddenly | **cuando era niño/a** — when I was a child |
| **una vez** — once | **todos los días** — every day (past routine) |
| **el otro día** — the other day | **siempre** — always (past habit) |
| **el año pasado** — last year | **nunca** — never (past habit) |
| **esta mañana** (finished) | **a menudo** — often (past) |
| **aquella tarde** (specific) | **cada verano** — each summer |

> ⚠️ Watch out: **mientras** can take *both* if there are two ongoing actions: *"Mientras **cantaba**, **bailaba**"* (two imperfectos).

---

## 4. The metaphor: **Photo vs Film** 📸🎞️

| **Indefinido = PHOTO** 📸 | **Imperfecto = FILM** 🎞️ |
|---|---|
| Captures a **specific moment** | Shows a **moving scene** |
| "I came, I saw, I conquered" (Julius Caesar) | "The sun was shining, people were laughing..." |
| Focuses on the **action** | Focuses on the **atmosphere** |
| One **click** and it's done | **Background** of the story |

**Example:**
> 📸 *"**Entré** en el pub, **pedí** una pinta y **saludé** a Dave."* (photos → indefinido)
>
> 🎞️ *"El pub **estaba** lleno, **sonaba** música de los 90 y la gente **bailaba**."* (scene → imperfecto)

---

## 5. Comparative table with English clues

| Context | Spanish | British English | Tense |
|---|---|---|---|
| Completed action | **Compré** el periódico | I **bought** the newspaper | **Indefinido** ✅ |
| Past habitual action | **Compraba** el periódico cada día | I **used to buy** / **would buy** the paper every day | **Imperfecto** 🔄 |
| Description / background | El cielo **estaba** gris | The sky **was** grey | **Imperfecto** 🌫️ |
| Punctual event interrupting | **Sonó** el teléfono | The phone **rang** | **Indefinido** 🔔 |
| Ongoing action | **Leía** el *Guardian* | I **was reading** the *Guardian* | **Imperfecto** 📖 |
| State / emotion (no limit) | **Quería** ir al pub | I **wanted** to go to the pub | **Imperfecto** 🍺 |
| Sudden change of state | **Se enfadó** | He **got angry** (sudden change) | **Indefinido** 😠💥 |

---

## 6. The star combination: **Imperfecto + Indefinido**

This is the most important narrative structure in Spanish. The **Imperfecto** sets the scene; the **Indefinido** delivers the main action.

### Formula:
> **Imperfecto** (scene / what *was happening*) → **CUANDO** → **Indefinido** (what *happened*)

### Everyday examples:

| Spanish | Mental translation (EN-UK) |
|---|---|
| **Tomaba** un café **cuando** **entró** mi jefe | I **was having** a coffee **when** my boss **walked in** |
| **Conducía** por la M25 **cuando** **se pinchó** la rueda | I **was driving** on the M25 **when** I **got** a puncture |
| **Veía** la tele **cuando** **cortaron** la luz | I **was watching** telly **when** the power **went out** |
| **Charlábamos** en la puerta del pub **cuando** **empezó** a llover | We **were chatting** outside the pub **when** it **started** raining |
| **Esperaba** el bus **cuando** **vi** a un viejo amigo | I **was waiting** for the bus **when** I **saw** an old mate |

### More examples (Spanish only):

- **Sonaba** el despertador, pero yo **seguía** durmiendo. De repente, **recordé** que **tenía** una reunión importante y **salté** de la cama.
- **Vivía** en un piso diminuto en Londres. Un día **decidí** mudarme al campo y **alquilé** una casa con jardín.
- **Siempre jugaba** al fútbol los sábados, pero aquel sábado **llovió** tanto que **cancelaron** el partido.

## 🏋️ Practice exercises — Indefinido or Imperfecto?

---

### Practice 1 — Central distinction

*Complete with the correct tense. Remember: Indefinido = completed action ✅ | Imperfecto = ongoing action / without limit 🌫️*

**1.** Ayer ______ (ir) al pub con mis colegas y ______ (pedir) una ronda de pintas.

*"Fui" and "pedí" are Indefinido because "ayer" marks a completed time period. Both actions have a clear beginning and end: they happened and they're done.*

**2.** De pequeña, ______ (vivir) en un pueblo cerca de Londres y ______ (ir) a un colegio muy pequeño.

*"Vivía" and "iba" are Imperfecto because they describe situations in the past without a defined limit. We don't know when they started or ended: they are the background scene.*

**3.** El otro día ______ (perder) el tren de las 8:15 y ______ (llegar) tarde al trabajo.

*"Perdí" and "llegué" are Indefinido because they are punctual, completed actions. "El otro día" points to a specific moment in the past.*

---

### Practice 2 — Mental hooks for Brits

*Use the "I was ___ing WHEN ___ happened" structure: the was/were -ing → Imperfecto; the ___ed → Indefinido.*

**4.** ______ (caminar) hacia la parada del bus CUANDO ______ (ver) un accidente de tráfico.

*"Caminaba" is Imperfecto (ongoing action = "I **was walking**"). "Vi" is Indefinido (punctual action = "I **saw**").*

**5.** ______ (tomar) una pinta tranquilamente en el pub CUANDO ______ (entrar) mi excompañero de piso.

*"Tomaba" is Imperfecto because it describes the background scene ("I **was having** a pint"). "Entró" is Indefinido because it interrupts the action ("he **walked in**").*

**6.** Nosotros ______ (hacer) cola para el concierto CUANDO ______ (empezar) a llover a cántaros.

*"Hacíamos" is Imperfecto ("we **were queuing**"), "empezó" is Indefinido ("it **started**" — action that interrupts).*

---

### Practice 3 — Trigger words

*Look at the clues: ayer, de repente, una vez → Indefinido. Mientras, cuando era niño, todos los días → Imperfecto.*

**7.** ______ (llover) mucho mientras ______ (conducir) por la M25 el viernes pasado.

*"Llovía" and "conducía" are Imperfecto because "mientras" indicates that both actions were in progress at the same time.*

**8.** De repente, el tren ______ (pararse) y todas las luces ______ (apagarse).

*"Se paró" and "se apagaron" are Indefinido because "de repente" indicates sudden, punctual actions.*

**9.** Cuando ______ (ser) niño, ______ (jugar) al fútbol en el parque todos los sábados.

*"Era" is Imperfecto because "cuando era niño" places the action in a period without a clear limit. "Jugaba" is also Imperfecto because "todos los sábados" indicates a habitual routine.*

---

### Practice 4 — Photo vs Film 📸🎞️

*Indefinido = PHOTO (concrete action, one click) | Imperfecto = FILM (scene, atmosphere, background)*

**10.** La fiesta de anoche ______ (ser) increíble. ______ (haber) música en directo y todo el mundo ______ (bailar).

*"Era", "había" and "bailaba" are all Imperfecto because they describe the atmosphere of the party — the background FILM.*

**11.** ______ (entrar) en la tienda, ______ (coger) el periódico y ______ (pagar) en la caja.

*"Entré", "cogí" and "pagué" are Indefinido — completed actions one after another, like three PHOTOS in sequence.*

**12.** El cielo ______ (estar) gris y ______ (llover) ligeramente cuando ______ (salir) de casa.

*"Estaba" and "llovía" are Imperfecto — they paint the landscape (FILM). "Salí" is Indefinido — punctual action (PHOTO).*

---

### Practice 5 — Comparative table with English clues

*Translate it mentally: "used to" / "was ___ing" → Imperfecto. "___ed (and it's done)" → Indefinido.*

**13.** Cuando ______ (vivir) en Mánchester, ______ (comprar) el *Guardian* cada mañana.

*"Vivía" and "compraba" are Imperfecto because they express a situation and a habitual routine — in English it would be "I **used to live**" / "I **used to buy**".*

**14.** El sábado pasado ______ (comprar) un billete de lotería, pero no ______ (ganar) nada.

*"Compré" and "gané" are Indefinido — completed actions at a specific moment. In English: "I **bought**" / "I **didn't win**" (and it's done).*

**15.** Mi hermana ______ (querer) mudarse a Edimburgo, pero al final ______ (decidir) quedarse en Londres.

*"Quería" is Imperfecto because it expresses a desire without a time limit ("she **wanted**", but we don't know if she still does). "Decidió" is Indefinido — punctual change of state ("she **decided**").*

---

### Practice 6 — The star combination: Imperfecto + Indefinido

*Imperfecto = the setting / what was happening. Indefinido = what happened (often with "cuando" or "de repente").*

**16.** ______ (leer) un libro en el tren cuando ______ (sonar) mi móvil.

*"Leía" is Imperfecto (ongoing action — the background). "Sonó" is Indefinido (action that interrupts — the foreground).*

**17.** ______ (esperar) el Tube en la estación de Oxford Circus cuando de repente ______ (oír) un anuncio por los altavoces.

*"Esperaba" is Imperfecto (background scene = "I **was waiting**"). "Oí" is Indefinido (punctual action = "I **heard**").*

**18.** Nosotros ______ (charlar) en la puerta del pub cuando ______ (ver) a un actor famoso.

*"Charlábamos" is Imperfecto (ongoing action = "we **were chatting**"). "Vimos" is Indefinido (concrete action that interrupts = "we **saw**").*
