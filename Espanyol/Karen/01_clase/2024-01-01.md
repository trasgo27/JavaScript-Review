## 👤 Personalities

> **George Osborne** (1971–): Chancellor of the Exchequer under Prime Minister David Cameron from 2010 to 2016. A leading figure in the Conservative Party, he was the architect of the UK's austerity programme following the 2008 financial crisis. His nickname "George Osborne" became synonymous with public spending cuts.

> **Edwin Poots** (1965–): Northern Irish unionist politician who served briefly as leader of the Democratic Unionist Party (DUP) in 2021. His tenure was the shortest of any DUP leader, lasting only 21 days. Previously served as Minister of Agriculture and Minister of Health in the Northern Ireland Executive.
